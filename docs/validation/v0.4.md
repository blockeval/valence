- Cross-region edge fraction: 0.4098
- Mean head agreement: 0.9740
- Mean finality agreement: 1.0000
- Stale-head attestations: 17
- Attempted effective p99 latency: 376.20 ms
- Delivered-message p99 latency: 212.00 ms
- Delivered/attempted p99 ratio: 0.5635

This ratio demonstrates why VALENCE reports both raw link samples and delivered
messages: redundant gossip paths preferentially deliver faster copies and can
mask the underlying link tail.

## Matched p99 sparse-overlay experiment

The low- and high-tail configurations have identical p50 and p95 targets. Only
p99 and its upper cap differ. A sparse degree-2, fanout-1 overlay minimizes
redundant-path masking.

Three paired seeds: 4, 9, and 14.

| Metric | Low p99 | High p99 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Target p50 | 500 ms | 500 ms |
| Target p95 | 3,000 ms | 3,000 ms |
| Target p99 | 5,000 ms | 15,000 ms |
| Observed attempted p99 | 4,728 ms | 13,419 ms |
| Delivered p99 | 4,728 ms | 13,419 ms |
| Mean head agreement | 0.5260 | 0.4948 |
| Mean stale-head attestations | 121.33 | 120.67 |
| Mean simulation completion | 192,119 ms | 194,635 ms |

The calibration manipulation is successful. Head agreement is lower on average
under the high-p99 condition in this small validation sweep, while stale-head
counts are nearly unchanged. These are engineering validation results, not the
final paper estimates; the ICDLT study should use more seeds, confidence
intervals, and several topology-redundancy levels.
